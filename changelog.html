0.1.0-beta.1 (early access)

12 November 2026

New

  • See everything connected to your Mac in one list that keeps itself up to date: USB devices and hubs, Bluetooth, Thunderbolt, displays, drives, the power adapter, the battery, cameras and audio, and even USB-C gadgets that only charge.
  • Know what each device is at a glance: its firmware, link speed and battery levels, a product photo and description kept on your Mac after the first lookup, and its maker, shown only when it is confirmed.
  • Spot what needs attention, such as a worn battery, outdated AirPods firmware, a drive on a slow USB link or a failing disk, with step-by-step fix instructions for each finding.
  • Eject or mount an external drive from the app, and remove a disconnected Bluetooth device from the list without unpairing it.
  • See where your Mac's disk space goes and how much each category could free; with Pro, clean a category after reviewing exactly what will be removed.
  • Check your Mac's Bluetooth health with a scored diagnostic (Pro) that recommends fixes and can apply two of them for you.
  • Choose between the app's own dark look and a native macOS look that follows light and dark mode.

Storage cleaning and the Bluetooth diagnostic are Pro features, unlocked by a one-time $4.99 payment. The app does not check for a licence yet, so this beta has them turned on.

Action needed

  • Grant Full Disk Access to Plugsquire again if you had given it to Retinue, the app's old name; your settings, device names, removed devices and stored product details carry over on their own at first launch.
