[proof needed — version number and release date]

New

  • Watch your Mac from the menu bar: the icon follows the most serious finding, and its menu lists what needs attention and each accessory's battery.
  • Get a notification once when a problem starts, such as a low battery, low disk space, a drive on a slow USB link, or a device connecting for the first time.
  • See every other device on your local network, grouped by kind, with its maker, model and the services it announces (Pro).
  • Check this Mac, its accessories and the devices on your network for published vulnerabilities, with the ones exploited in attacks first and the update that fixes them (Pro).
  • Check one network device's open ports when you choose to, and see your Wi-Fi network's signal, channel and security next to the networks nearby (Pro).
  • Find what fills your disk on a map, and move large or long-unchanged files to the Trash after reviewing them (Pro).
  • Remove an app together with the files it left in your Library (Pro).
  • Look back at what connected and disconnected in a searchable connection history.
  • Keep an eye on accessory batteries with a small and a medium desktop widget.
  • Let Plugsquire update itself, always after asking you, and open it at login.
  • With ssh-fleet-monitor installed separately, see your servers' health and risk next to your Mac.

Improved

  • Downloaded releases open on the first launch, also offline: they are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
  • Remembered AirPods and other Bluetooth devices keep their last known battery levels, marked with how old they are.
  • More devices on the local network are named instead of listed as unknown.
  • Settings › Alerts shows which alert each threshold belongs to.

Fixed

  • A local network scan is no longer reported as refused when you allow access while it runs.
  • Low battery and low disk space alerts arrive once, even when a reading hovers at the threshold.
  • Clicking an alert while Plugsquire is closed now opens the device it is about.

0.1.0-beta.3 (early access)

15 November 2026

New

  • Find the Git worktrees under a folder and delete the ones Git confirms have nothing uncommitted and nothing unpushed (Pro).

Improved

  • Plugsquire asks for Full Disk Access only when a feature needs it, and names what it could not read.
  • After a clean, only the cleaned category is measured again, so the storage breakdown updates sooner.

0.1.0-beta.2 (early access)

12 November 2026

Improved

  • Plugsquire now runs on Intel Macs as well as Apple silicon, on macOS 14 or later on both. Intel support is new in this beta and has not yet been tried on an Intel Mac, so if you use one, please report any problem to contact@luongnv.com. Detecting power-only devices on USB-C ports still needs an Apple silicon Mac.

Action needed

  • If you installed beta 1 and granted Full Disk Access, grant it again in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Full Disk Access after replacing the app.

0.1.0-beta.1 (early access)

12 November 2026

New

  • See everything connected to your Mac in one list that keeps itself up to date: USB devices and hubs, Bluetooth, Thunderbolt, displays, drives, the power adapter, the battery, cameras and audio, and even USB-C gadgets that only charge.
  • Know what each device is at a glance: its firmware, link speed and battery levels, a product photo and description kept on your Mac after the first lookup, and its maker, shown only when it is confirmed.
  • Spot what needs attention, such as a worn battery, outdated AirPods firmware, a drive on a slow USB link or a failing disk, with step-by-step fix instructions for each finding.
  • Eject or mount an external drive from the app, and remove a disconnected Bluetooth device from the list without unpairing it.
  • See where your Mac's disk space goes and how much each category could free; with Pro, clean a category after reviewing exactly what will be removed.
  • Check your Mac's Bluetooth health with a scored diagnostic (Pro) that recommends fixes and can apply two of them for you.
  • Choose between the app's own dark look and a native macOS look that follows light and dark mode.

Storage cleaning and the Bluetooth diagnostic are Pro features, unlocked by a one-time Pro payment (see Pricing for the current price and Pro list). The app does not check for a licence yet, so this beta has them turned on.

Action needed

  • Grant Full Disk Access to Plugsquire again if you had given it to Retinue, the app's old name; your settings, device names, removed devices and stored product details carry over on their own at first launch.
