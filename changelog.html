0.1.0 (early access)

12 November 2026

New

  • See every device connected to your Mac in one list: USB with its hub tree, Bluetooth, Thunderbolt, displays, storage, the power adapter, the internal battery, cameras and audio.
  • Check each device's identity: vendor and product IDs, serial, firmware, link speed, power granted versus power wanted, and battery levels.
  • Get an audit of every device, with the evidence for each finding, numbered fix steps and a button to the right System Settings pane where one exists.
  • Spot power-only accessories charging over USB-C, and give them a name.
  • Read product names, photos, descriptions and firmware history from public sources, stored on your Mac so each name is asked about once.
  • Remove a remembered Bluetooth device from the list without unpairing it.
  • Eject or mount the volumes of an external drive from the app.
  • See where the internal disk's space goes, by category, with an estimate of how much could be freed.
  • Clean a storage category after reviewing exactly what will be removed (Pro).
  • Score your Mac's Bluetooth health and apply the confirmed fixes it recommends (Pro).
  • Switch between the Signature look and the Native macOS look.

Action needed

  • If you used the app under its earlier name, Retinue: your settings, device names and stored details carry over on first launch, but you need to grant Full Disk Access again.
