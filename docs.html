Requirements

  • macOS 14 or later.
  • An Apple silicon Mac. The current beta build runs on Apple silicon only, and detecting power-only devices on USB-C ports needs one in any build.
  • An internet connection is optional. It is used only for "Online details".

Install

Plugsquire is in early access. Request an early build by email.

  1. Unzip Plugsquire-0.1.0-beta.1.zip and move Plugsquire.app to Applications. This build runs on Apple silicon Macs only.
  2. Allow the first launch. The build is signed ad hoc and not notarized, so macOS blocks it. On macOS 15 or later, try to open the app, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click "Open Anyway". On macOS 14, Control-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

Plugsquire is not on the Mac App Store: the App Store sandbox blocks the hardware reads it depends on.

First run

  1. Open Plugsquire. It scans every bus at launch, again every two minutes, and whenever a USB device is plugged in or removed.
  2. Read the list. Devices that need attention are flagged; observations that need no action are labelled "Nothing to fix".
  3. Select a device to see its identity and findings. Each finding says what to do in one line, then gives numbered steps and its source.
  4. Select the internal disk to see where its space goes. The breakdown is measured when you first open it, and again when you press "Measure again".
  5. Open "Bluetooth" under Diagnostics to score the radio's health (Pro). It reads five minutes of logs, which takes a few seconds.

Rescan at any time with ⌘R.

Troubleshooting

  • The Trash or iPhone backups show "Not measured", or a total reads "At least". Grant Plugsquire Full Disk Access, then press "Measure again".
  • A Bluetooth or power-only device change is not shown yet. These are picked up by the two-minute rescan. Press ⌘R to rescan now.
  • A device shows no maker. A Bluetooth device can announce any vendor ID, so Plugsquire names a maker only when something else agrees. Type the maker in yourself.
  • Some product photos or descriptions are missing. When Wikipedia limits requests, Plugsquire waits, stops asking for that pass, and tries the remaining names on the next rescan.
  • Deleted space does not show as free yet. macOS may keep it in a local Time Machine snapshot for a few minutes.
  • A drive will not eject. macOS refused, for example because the drive is in use. Plugsquire shows the reason macOS gave.

Uninstall

Delete the app, then delete ~/Library/Application Support/Plugsquire/ and ~/Library/Caches/Plugsquire/ to remove the stored details and cached answers.
